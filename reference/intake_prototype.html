Find the right free assessment for your customer

Work through these discovery questions with your customer, ideally with their security, identity and AI leads on the call. The answers decide which Cisco assessments fit, and in what order. It takes about 8 minutes.

ISAIdentity Security Assessment

  • Human, non-human and AI agent identities, on a sovereign Cisco Identity Intelligence instance
  • Read-only API connection to the customer's identity providers. Nothing is deployed and production isn't affected.
  • Findings on IAM hygiene, dormant and privileged accounts, MFA strength and gaps, and unusual activity
  • Agent and non-human visibility is basic compared with AVA
  • Data can take up to 14 days to stabilise after connection

AVAAgent Visibility Assessment

  • AI agent identities only, in more depth than ISA
  • Integrates with CrowdStrike Falcon or Microsoft Defender to find agents
  • Runs on a US-hosted Cisco Identity Intelligence instance
  • One-week assessment with a PDF readout

RTAI Red Teaming Assessment

  • Automated adversarial testing of up to three models, agents or AI applications
  • Jailbreaks, prompt injection, harmful output, data and system-prompt leakage, and tool misuse for agents
  • A report mapped to the OWASP Top 10 for LLMs and MITRE ATLAS, and a readout

    ISA or AVA?

    Both run on Cisco Identity Intelligence. They differ in what they see, where the data lives and how long they take.

    ISAIdentity Security AssessmentAVAAgent Visibility Assessment

    Agent and NHI depthBasicDeep (agents)

    How the recommendation works

    Each assessment gets its own score out of 100 after an entry check. ISA needs a supported identity provider and read-only access. AVA needs AI agents in use, CrowdStrike Falcon or Microsoft Defender, and acceptance of US hosting. RT needs AI the customer builds or runs, with a reachable endpoint. Then 70 or more is fast-track, 40–69 goes to Cisco review, and under 40 is nurture. A personal email address for the customer contact caps every track at review.

    When both identity assessments qualify, AVA is recommended on its own unless there are at least two human or non-human identity gaps, in which case both run. When agents are in use without clear visibility of their identities, the identity assessment goes before RT, so the agents are found before they're tested.
